Terms of use

Drive Inbox is a personal utility for copying authorized Google Drive documents into a Markdown inbox.

Purpose and availability

The app is operated for its owner's personal use. This website is informational; it does not offer public accounts, hosted document processing, or a public import service. There is no service-level commitment or guaranteed availability.

Authorized use

Only authorize an account and import content you are permitted to access, copy, and store. Read access does not transfer ownership of documents or remove obligations to other content owners.

Limitations

Imports can fail because of authorization problems, unsupported formats, conversion errors, or service changes. Conversion can lose formatting or content. This is not a backup system or a two-way synchronization service. Later source edits and deletions are not necessarily reflected in imported files.

The current implementation can replace an existing local file if another import resolves to the same destination filename. Keep appropriate backups and review important imports.

Your responsibilities

The operator is responsible for protecting credentials and server access, monitoring scheduled runs, checking imported results, and configuring retention, synchronization, and backups. Do not submit secrets or private documents through this public site.

Privacy and stopping use

See the privacy policy for data handling and deletion details. To stop future imports, disable the schedule and revoke Google access. Previously imported files require separate deletion.

Independent tool

Drive Inbox is not a Google product and is not endorsed by Google. References to Google Drive and Obsidian describe services used in the owner's workflow.

Draft status

These terms are a description of the intended personal-use arrangement, not a claim that Google has reviewed or approved the app. The operator must review this draft before publishing it.
